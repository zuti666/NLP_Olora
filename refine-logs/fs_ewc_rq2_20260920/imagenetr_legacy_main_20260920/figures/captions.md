## Figure_C_stage_aware

Existing ImageNet-A checkpoints, training seed 0, re-evaluated in current full FP32. These are supplementary ImageNet-A measurements, separate from the historical ImageNet-R main table. The historical SGD+EWC sequence uses SGD for both the Task0 anchor and subsequent learning; the historical GAM+EWC sequence uses GAM for both. Thus the future optimizers differ. $T$ is the total number of learned tasks, $T=t_{\mathrm{code}}+1$: $T=1$ is the Task0 anchor, $T=2$ is after Task1, and $T=10$ is after Task9. Open markers indicate Task0 anchors; the dotted separator divides the anchor from subsequent learning. Co-located zero anchors are drawn with an outer SGD circle and inner GAM diamond at the same coordinate. (a) Old Task0 test accuracy and (b) signed old-task test cross-entropy damage $D_T=L_0^{\mathrm{test}}(W_{T-1})-L_0^{\mathrm{test}}(W_0)$, relative to each method's own anchor. Both use a fixed Task0 20-class head and the same 307 deterministic test examples throughout. Negative damage indicates a loss decrease. These are local Task0 accuracies, not accuracies among all seen classes. All 20 actual checkpoint rows are shown without smoothing, clipping, or monotonicity enforcement. GAM damage peaks at $T=7$ (0.073354) and decreases to 0.034347 at $T=10$; SGD damage also decreases at $T=2$ and $T=5$. (c) Measured chord-averaged directional sensitivity only at $T=10$, $\bar S_{10}=I_{10}/r_{10}^2$, where $I_{10}=\int_0^1\Delta W_{10}^{\top}\widehat F_{\mathrm{emp}}(W_0+s\Delta W_{10})\Delta W_{10}\,ds$ and $\Delta W_{10}=W_9-W_0$. Here $\widehat F_{\mathrm{emp}}=N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N g_i g_i^{\top}$ uses unscaled per-example CE gradients on the same $N=256$ stratified Task0 training examples, with the Task0 head fixed and no trace normalization, floor, or regularization. Nine composite-Simpson nodes evaluate the Task0-to-Task9 effective-weight straight segment, not the actual optimizer-step trajectory. The recorded 5-versus-9-node quadrature checks pass the 5\% gate, and the matched-subset numerical bound checks pass their 2\% tolerance. There are no intermediate-stage sensitivity measurements, so only two endpoint bars are drawn. (d) Actual cumulative displacement $r_T=\|W_{T-1}-W_0\|_2$ from each sequence's own Task0 anchor, in the joint ambient coordinates of 24 effective Q/V $BA$ matrices with scaling 1. This is a displacement norm, not a sum of step lengths or a damage-tolerance radius. Lines connect measured task checkpoints as visual guides. There is one training run per method; the figure provides no uncertainty estimate across runs or isolated causal effect of the Task0 anchor.

## Figure_r_vs_learning_stage

Existing ImageNet-A checkpoints, training seed 0, re-evaluated in current full FP32. These are supplementary ImageNet-A measurements, separate from the historical ImageNet-R main table. The historical SGD+EWC sequence uses SGD for both the Task0 anchor and subsequent learning; the historical GAM+EWC sequence uses GAM for both. Thus the future optimizers differ. $T$ is the total number of learned tasks, $T=t_{\mathrm{code}}+1$: $T=1$ is the Task0 anchor, $T=2$ is after Task1, and $T=10$ is after Task9. Open markers indicate Task0 anchors; the dotted separator divides the anchor from subsequent learning. Co-located zero anchors are drawn with an outer SGD circle and inner GAM diamond at the same coordinate. Actual cumulative effective-weight displacement $r_T=\|W_{T-1}-W_0\|_2$ is measured from each sequence's own Task0 anchor in the joint ambient coordinates of 24 effective Q/V $BA$ matrices with scaling 1. All 20 original checkpoint rows are drawn at their actual $T$ and displacement values, without smoothing or coordinate offsets; connecting lines are visual guides between checkpoints. This is neither the sum of successive step lengths nor an estimated damage-tolerance radius. At $T=10$, the measured displacements are 1.197584 for SGD+EWC and 1.400473 for GAM+EWC. Each curve is one training run (seed 0), so no uncertainty across runs is estimated. The differing continuation optimizers prevent an isolated causal comparison of the anchors.
