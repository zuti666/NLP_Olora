Training-neighborhood diagnostics on the formal three-seed ImageNet-A cohort (ViT-B/16 with rank-10 Q/V LoRA). Panels show average positive training loss change, training accuracy (%), empirical Fisher trace, and training tolerance radius. The first axis uses simplified average-damage notation; its statistic is $G(r)=K^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^K\max\{N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N[\ell_i(\theta+ru_k)-\ell_i(\theta)],0\}$, with N=1,259 training examples and K=100 common directions. CE is averaged over samples before taking each direction's positive part; this is neither summed training loss nor the mean of individual positive sample changes. Loss/accuracy use 81 radii from 0 to 20 in steps of 0.25. Fisher uses the same Task-0 anchors and effective Q/V coordinates, a fixed 256-example subset, the first 16 paired directions, and seven measured radii (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20); it is the unnormalized true-label empirical trace, without an EWC coefficient. Fisher connecting segments are guides. The origin is measured once. The fourth panel uses the exact prefix inverse $\hat r_\epsilon=\max\{r_j:\max_{i\le j}G(r_i)\le\epsilon\}$, computed per seed before averaging. Its linear axis labels the originally stored thresholds 0.001, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05. Additional thresholds 0.0025, 0.0075, 0.015, 0.03, 0.04 are derived from the same measured loss grid by postprocessing, not by new model evaluations. Markers show selected thresholds that lie within each displayed line; the 0.01 guide is retained. All threshold values, including omitted capped markers, are exported. Each method's line stops at the first threshold where all three seeds reach the scan cap r=20; the later fully capped horizontal tail is omitted. Some seeds are censored earlier. Endpoints at 20 are scan limits, not identified true boundaries. Curves and bands are mean and one sample SD over training seeds 0/1/2. EWCLoRA uses blue solid circles and FS-EWCLoRA orange dashed diamonds. These finite-direction training diagnostics do not guarantee every sample/direction or improved test tolerance.
