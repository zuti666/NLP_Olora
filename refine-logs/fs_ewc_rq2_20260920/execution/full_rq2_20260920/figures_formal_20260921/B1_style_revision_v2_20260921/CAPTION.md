Training-neighborhood diagnostics on the formal three-seed ImageNet-A cohort (ViT-B/16 with rank-10 Q/V LoRA). From left to right: average positive training loss change, training accuracy (%), empirical Fisher trace, and training tolerance radius. The first axis uses the simplified average-damage notation $\overline{D_t(\boldsymbol{\theta},\boldsymbol{\Delta})}$; the plotted statistic remains $G(r)=100^{-1}\sum_k\max\{L_0(\theta+ru_k)-L_0(\theta),0\}$, with positive parts taken before averaging, and is not changed to signed mean damage. Loss and accuracy use all 1,259 Task-0 training examples, 100 common unit directions, and 81 measured radii (0 to 20 in steps of 0.25). Fisher uses a fixed 256-example training subset, the first 16 common directions, and seven measured radii (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20); it is the unnormalized true-label empirical trace $\operatorname{tr}F_0^{\mathrm{emp}}$ in the same effective Q/V weight coordinates, without the EWC coefficient. Fisher line segments are visual guides, not additional measurements. The origin is evaluated once. The fourth panel is the exact finite-grid prefix inverse $\hat r_\epsilon=\max\{r_j:\max_{i\le j}G(r_i)\le\epsilon\}$, computed per seed before aggregation. Markers identify the saved thresholds 0.001, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02; the vertical dotted line highlights 0.01. Each method's line and band stop at the first threshold where all three seeds reach the scan cap r=20; the subsequent fully capped horizontal tail is omitted for display, with all values retained in the CSVs. Some seeds are already censored before this endpoint. The endpoints at 20 are scan limits, not identified true boundaries or proof of equal tolerance. Curves and bands are means and one sample SD over training seeds 0/1/2. These are training-side finite-direction diagnostics, not guarantees for all perturbations or test-side improvements. EWCLoRA is blue solid with circles and FS-EWCLoRA orange dashed with diamonds in every panel.
