\caption{Training-neighborhood diagnostics on the formal three-seed ImageNet-A cohort (ViT-B/16 with rank-10 Q/V LoRA). From left to right: mean positive training CE damage (the first axis abbreviates the underlying damage quantity as $D_t(\boldsymbol{\theta},\boldsymbol{\Delta})$; the plotted aggregation remains $G=\mathbb{E}_u[\max(D_t,0)]$ with the positive part taken before averaging), training accuracy (%), raw empirical Fisher trace, and empirical training tolerance radius. Damage and accuracy use all 1,259 Task-0 training examples, 100 paired unit directions, and 81 radii from 0 to 20 in steps of 0.25. Fisher uses a fixed 256-example training subset, the first 16 common directions, and seven measured radii (0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20); it is the unnormalized true-label per-example gradient-square sum, without the EWC coefficient. Segments between Fisher nodes are visual guides. The fourth panel is computed from the first panel's per-seed damage data: $\hat r_\epsilon=\max\{r_j:\max_{i\le j}G(r_i)\le\epsilon\}$, where $G(r)=100^{-1}\sum_k[L_0(\theta+ru_k)-L_0(\theta)]_+$. It is the exact finite-grid step function, with no radius interpolation or new model evaluations. Markers show the saved thresholds 0.001, 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02; the vertical dotted line and error bars highlight 0.01. At the saved threshold 0.05, all seeds of both methods reach the scan limit 20; the plateaus at 20 are right-censored and do not establish equality of the unknown boundaries beyond the scan. Some individual seeds reach the cap before 0.05; all plateaus at 20 reflect this cap. Curves and bands are means and one sample SD over training seeds 0/1/2; tolerance radii are inverted per seed before aggregation. The origin is evaluated once. The tolerance panel is a threshold summary of damage, not independent evidence or a guarantee for every direction in a ball. All four panels concern training data and do not establish improved test-neighborhood tolerance.

Across all four panels, EWCLoRA uses a blue solid line with circles, and FS-EWCLoRA an orange dashed line with diamonds; legends use the same encodings. The concise Fisher label still denotes the unnormalized empirical trace.}
