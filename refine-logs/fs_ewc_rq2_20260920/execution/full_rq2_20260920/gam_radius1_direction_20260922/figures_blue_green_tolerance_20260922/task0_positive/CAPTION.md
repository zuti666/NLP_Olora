Two-method fixed-direction diagnostic using the positive gradient of old Task0 training CE. Blue is EWCLoRA; green is the newly trained FS-EWCLoRA with GAM probe radius 0.05 and outer radius 1.0. Formal ImageNet-A, ViT-B/16 with rank-10 Q/V LoRA, training seeds 0/1/2. Each anchor has its own direction, computed once and held fixed for r=0,0.5,...,5 in effective Q/V weights. All panels evaluate the old Task0 model with its own fixed old20 head. Panels 1 and 2 show mean signed Task0 training CE change and accuracy (%) on all 1259 training images, with sample-SD shading across three seeds. Symmetric SD ribbons are descriptive and can extend below physical bounds. Panel 3 uses a linear vertical axis for raw empirical Fisher trace on the same 256 old training samples, with arithmetic mean curves and faint individual-seed curves; no Fisher SD band, normalization, floor or EWC lambda. Panel 4 uses the reference measured_tolerances function: for each seed, take the prefix maximum of positive loss change and return the last measured radius before its first value above epsilon. Average these three grid radii after inversion; no SD band is drawn in panel 4. Thresholds are 0.25,0.5,1,1.5,2,2.5,3 mean CE units per image. All displayed results are bracketed by adjacent measured radii separated by 0.5. The reference thresholds 0.005--0.05 all give zero here and are preserved in the CSV. No interpolation or new model evaluation is used. Connecting lines are visual guides; tolerance applies to the measured fixed ray, not all directions in a ball. Any future right-censored mean would be left empty rather than averaging only uncensored seeds.
