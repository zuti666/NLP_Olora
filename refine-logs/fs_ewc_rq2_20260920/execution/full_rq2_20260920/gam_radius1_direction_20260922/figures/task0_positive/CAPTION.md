Controlled GAM training-radius intervention on formal ImageNet-A (ViT-B/16, rank-10 Q/V LoRA; seeds 0, 1, 2). The four curves are the existing EWCLoRA control, existing FS-EWCLoRA, and two newly trained FS-EWCLoRA anchors. The ordered pair in each FS-EWCLoRA legend is (rho, r_train): the normalized-gradient probe radius and the gradient-difference ascent radius used during GAM training. The existing pair is (0.05, 0.2); the two interventions are (0.05, 1) and (1, 1). Both active as1 aliases are changed consistently. All other training settings, the pretrained frozen backbone, fresh LoRA/head initialization, 50-epoch training budget, and paired data order are held fixed. These training radii are measured in trainable LoRA-factor/classifier coordinates and are distinct from the horizontal-axis diagnostic radius r in effective Q/V weights. At each trained anchor, compute once the positive gradient of mean Task0 training CE over all 1259 images, globally normalize the 24 effective Q/V gradient matrices, and hold that one direction fixed through theta(r)=theta(0)+r*u. Each method and intervention uses its own newly computed direction. The horizontal grid is the 11 measured points 0, 0.5, ..., 5; old controls are selected from their completed denser scans. Left: signed Task0 training CE change D0(r)=L0(theta(r))-L0(theta(0)); the overbar denotes the mean over three training seeds. L0 is mean CE per image over all 1259 Task0 training images. Negative loss changes are retained without clipping. Middle: accuracy (%) on the same images. Right: raw empirical Fisher trace, mean_i ||gradient CE_i||^2 on the unchanged 256-image Task0 training subset, in the same effective Q/V coordinates. The original Task0 20-class classifier and all other model state remain fixed throughout each scan. No extra Fisher normalization, floor, or EWC lambda is applied. Lines and shading show arithmetic mean and one sample standard deviation (ddof=1) across three training seeds, not SEM or confidence intervals. Connecting segments are guides and introduce no measured points. The separate CSV includes absolute CE, positive-part damage and test metrics, while these panels show signed training damage. A separate origin table reports the fit at every trained anchor. A smaller CE increment alone does not imply a lower absolute perturbed CE or a better-trained anchor; both quantities must be inspected before a robustness claim. Small finite-difference probes verify the requested gradient sign; no sign or monotonicity condition is imposed on finite-radius Task0 loss changes. This fixed effective-weight ray is a diagnostic, not an iterative optimization trajectory or a completed Task1 learning experiment.
