\caption{Figure B1. Training-neighborhood diagnostics for the formal ImageNet-A seed0 Task0 anchors. From left to right: mean positive empirical loss increase, absolute Task0 training accuracy, raw empirical Fisher trace, and the largest measured prefix-safe tolerance radius. The first panel shows $G_0(r)=K^{-1}\sum_k[D_k(r)]_+$, where $D_k(r)=L_0(W_0+ru_k)-L_0(W_0)$; the axis notation $\overline{[D_0]_+}$ explicitly denotes the positive part before averaging directions. Loss and accuracy use all 1,259 old-task training examples, 50 paired directions, and 107 measured radii per method. Fisher uses 256 fixed training examples, 16 paired directions, and 42 measured radii per method. It averages true-label per-example gradient squared norms in the 24 effective Q/V matrices, without EWC lambda scaling or trace normalization. Anchors were trained with rank-10 LoRA; these diagnostic perturbations lie in the full effective-weight space and are not constrained to remain rank 10. Tolerance is computed directly from the first panel: $\hat r_\epsilon=\max\{r_j:\max_{i\leq j}G_0(r_i)\leq\epsilon\}$, using the measured grid without interpolation. All eleven thresholds through epsilon=0.1 are retained. Radii 21–24 were not measured; connections between measured points are visual guides, and the grid gap limits tolerance resolution. The measured r=17 Fisher peak is retained. These are single-seed direction means; no cross-seed uncertainty band is shown.}
