Historical ImageNet-A seed0 Task0 neighborhood measurements for EWCLoRA (SGD) and FS-EWCLoRA (visual GAM). From left: directional mean positive Task0 training CE damage, directional mean training accuracy (%), and an approximate reconstruction of the fixed unnormalized anchor Fisher proxy trace. The third panel displays $\widehat T_{\rm raw}(r)=c_0\,2d\overline Q_F(r)/r^2$ for $r>0$, with $\overline Q_F(r)=K^{-1}\sum_k\frac12r^2u_k^\top\widetilde F_0u_k$, where $K=100$, $d=14{,}155{,}776$, and $\widetilde F_0$ is the stored diagonal anchor Fisher proxy with trace approximately one. This proxy squares minibatch-mean CE gradients, is fixed throughout the radius scan and has no EWC lambda multiplier; it is not raw per-example empirical Fisher trace at the perturbed point. The first two panels use all 81 radii from 0 to 20 in steps of 0.25. The third uses the 80 positive radii: its r=0 value is undefined and omitted. Markers are shown every 2.5 units. Fresh isotropic directions were drawn at each radius; cross-method direction pairing is unverified. All three quantities are read from the same historical training-split radius/direction records; train inputs use one cached draw of stochastic training augmentation, and each method retains its own fixed Task0 head. Positive damage is $[L_0(\theta_0+ru)-L_0(\theta_0)]_+$. Weight drift is the joint Frobenius radius over 24 effective Q/V matrices (14,155,776 coordinates). The external scales c0 are the recorded pre-normalization traces from the matching Task0 runs: 29.875783436 for EWCLoRA and 26.164499521 for FS-EWCLoRA. Restoration is approximate at floating-point normalization precision. Each point estimates the same fixed anchor proxy trace; fluctuations are Monte Carlo/numerical variation. The method-scale difference comes from those recorded anchor traces, not newly discovered radius-dependent sensitivity. This historical plot uses a different measurement protocol from the deterministic raw-Fisher diagnostic; its curves are not mixed with those new measurements.
