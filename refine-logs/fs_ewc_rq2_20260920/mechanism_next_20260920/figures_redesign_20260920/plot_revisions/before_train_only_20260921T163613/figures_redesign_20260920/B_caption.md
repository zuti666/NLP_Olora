**Figure B. Task0 neighborhood diagnostics for EWCLoRA and FS-EWCLoRA.** Existing ImageNet-A seed0 checkpoints are evaluated with deterministic transforms and each method's fixed 20-class Task0 head. Radius $r$ is the joint Frobenius distance from its own Task0 anchor across 24 effective Q/V weight matrices. The same $K=16$ unit directions are shared across methods and positive radii; $r=0$ reuses one origin per method. For each direction, positive damage is $[L(w_0+ru)-L(w_0)]_+$, using mean cross-entropy on 1,259 Task0 training or 307 Task0 test images. Panels (a) and (b) show its directional mean $G_{\mathrm{mean}}$ and empirical 95th percentile $G_{95}$, respectively. Panel (c) shows the mean and $Q_{95}$ of raw empirical Fisher traces across directions, estimated on a fixed subset of 256 training images without normalization, flooring, or regularization. Quantiles use linear interpolation. Panel (d) gives the prefix-safe tolerance radius $\hat r_\epsilon$, the largest sampled radius for which $G_{\mathrm{mean}}\leq\epsilon$ at every sampled radius up to that point. Triangles mark right-censoring at the maximum probed radius, 20; nested markers preserve exact method ties. Solid/filled and dashed/open denote train and test in (a), (b), and (d). Radius axes in (a–c) and damage axes in (a–b) use symmetric-log scales with linear thresholds 0.5 and $10^{-4}$; (d) uses logarithmic $\epsilon$. Connecting lines guide the eye; these finite-direction summaries are not confidence intervals or worst-case guarantees.
