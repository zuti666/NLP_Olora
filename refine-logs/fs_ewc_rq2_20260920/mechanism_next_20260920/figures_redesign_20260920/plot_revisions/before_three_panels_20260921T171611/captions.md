Figure B. Training-set neighborhood diagnostics on historical ImageNet-A seed0 checkpoints. From left to right: mean positive Task0 cross-entropy increase; mean Task0 training accuracy (%); mean raw empirical Fisher trace; and the mean-damage empirical tolerance radius. Weight drift is the perturbation radius: the joint Frobenius distance across 24 effective Q/V weight matrices from each method's own Task0 anchor. The measured radii are 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20. The same 16 unit directions are shared between methods and all positive radii; radius zero is one reused anchor. Loss and accuracy use all 1,259 Task0 training images with deterministic transforms and each method's fixed 20-class head; Fisher uses the same fixed 256 training images, true-label per-example gradients, without trace normalization, flooring or regularization. Its trace sums squared gradients over all 14,155,776 effective-weight coordinates, averages over samples and directions, and is not multiplied by the EWC coefficient. The first three panels show means across directions. Absolute accuracy retains the methods' different anchor accuracies. The loss-damage notation is $D_t(\boldsymbol{\theta},\boldsymbol{\Delta})=L_t(\boldsymbol{\theta}+\boldsymbol{\Delta})-L_t(\boldsymbol{\theta})$; the first panel displays $G(r)=K^{-1}\sum_k[D_0(w_0,ru_k)]_+$, the mean positive damage, not the absolute loss. The tolerance radius $\hat r_\epsilon$ is the largest sampled radius such that $G(r_i)\leq\epsilon$ at every sampled radius up to it. Both methods have identical tolerance values at the five preset thresholds, shown with nested markers. This panel is a threshold summary of the first panel's measured damage curve, not an independent measurement. Triangles mark right-censoring at the maximum scanned radius 20: the measured tolerance reaches the scan limit. All axes are linear; lines guide the eye between measured points. Only training means are plotted, with no test curves or Q95 series. These finite-direction, single-seed diagnostics are not worst-case guarantees.
